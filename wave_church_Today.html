How the psalm unfolds
vv. 1–6
God calls His people before the whole world.

He tells His people to come before Him with thanksgiving and without misunderstanding who He is.

He calls His people to turn away from sinful actions.
I
4Then he appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the LORD, to give thanks, praise, and worship the LORD God of Israel. 5Asaph was the chief, and next to him was Zechariah … and Jeiel. They played stringed instruments and harps, while Asaph played the cymbals with strength.

II
1The Mighty One, the LORD God, has spoken and called the earth from the rising of the sun to its going down. 2Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God has shone forth. 3Our God shall come and shall not keep silent; a consuming fire goes before Him, and a mighty storm surrounds Him. 4He calls to the heavens above and to the earth, that He may judge His people: 5Gather My saints together to Me, those who have made a covenant with Me by sacrifice. 6Let the heavens declare His righteousness, for God Himself is Judge. Selah.
